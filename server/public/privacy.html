Privacy & your data

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short

  • CheckMate never sells your data and never uses it for advertising.
  • Uploaded files never leave your computer. Only their text is sent to be checked, and that text isn't kept.
  • The pages you scan aren't linked to you.
  • You can delete your data at any time from the CheckMate menu.

Because scanned page text goes into the shared cache, avoid scanning pages that show your own private information, such as a page you're signed in to that shows your account details.

How your documents are handled

  • Files you upload are opened and read on your own computer, in the browser. The file itself is never sent.
  • Only the document's text is sent, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), to be checked. It is used for that check and then discarded.
  • “This flag is wrong” reports can't be sent for uploaded documents, so no quote from them is ever copied out.
  • Every finding comes with the exact words it's about, which are checked against your document, so nothing is made up.

Who checks the text

Today, CheckMate's analysis runs entirely on CheckMate's own server. No outside AI company receives your text. If that changes, this page will name the company and what it receives before it happens, and CheckMate will never allow your text to be used to train AI models.

CheckMate's server runs on Vercel and its database on Neon, which store the data listed above for CheckMate.

Deleting your data

  • Your reports and scan history: click the CheckMate icon, open the menu under your name, and choose Delete my data. Today's scan count is kept until it's cleared automatically, so the daily limit stays fair.
  • An uploaded document: press Forget this document on the upload page while it's open.
  • Your name and invite code: email newtzdesign2020@gmail.com and your invite, with everything linked to it, will be removed.
  • Data on your computer: removing the CheckMate extension from Chrome deletes everything it saved in your browser.
